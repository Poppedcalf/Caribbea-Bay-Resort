## Good to know

How far is it from Kariba Airport and Harare?

About 13 to 15 km from Kariba Airport and 3 km from the Dam Wall. Harare is roughly 3.5 to 4 hours away by road.

Can I camp at the resort?

Yes. There are 35 lakefront terrace sites for up to 70 guests, with serviced tents or space to pitch your own. Campers can use the resort’s pools and facilities.

What is there for children?

A children’s pool with the Supa Tube water slide, beach volleyball by the water and family rooms that sleep up to five. Children must be accompanied by an adult at the pools.

Can you host a workshop or summit?

Yes. Four climate-controlled venues seat 10 to 120 delegates, with projection and AV. Use the conference planner above to send us a brief.
